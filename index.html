Sample workbook format & usage notes

Sheet: Prices

Required: timestamp (or date), symbol, close (or price). Optional: open, high, low, volume, adj_close.

timestamp    symbol  close    open     high     low      volume
2024-01-02   AAPL    185.20   184.50   186.10   183.90   72543210
2024-01-02   MSFT    374.85   373.10   376.20   372.50   24581000
2024-01-03   AAPL    184.10   185.00   185.30   183.20   68902100

Sheet: Trades

Required: timestamp, symbol, side (BUY/SELL, B/S, Long/Short, Bot/Sld, Bought/Sold), qty (or quantity/shares), price (execution price). Optional: strategy, notes.

timestamp    symbol  side  qty  price   strategy  notes
2024-01-05   AAPL    BUY   100  184.40  Momentum  Initial entry
2024-01-13   AAPL    BUY    50  183.00  Momentum  Add on weakness
2024-01-22   AAPL    SELL   75  184.37  Momentum  Partial profit
2024-02-07   AAPL    SELL   75  188.32  Momentum  Close remaining

Timestamp formats accepted

ISO 8601: 2024-01-05 or 2024-01-05T09:30:00
US date: 01/05/2024 or 1/5/2024
Excel serial number (auto-detected)

P&L Method — Average Cost

Each BUY updates the running average cost: avgCost = (avgCost × pos + execPrice × qty) / (pos + qty).
Each SELL realises P&L: P&L = (execPrice − avgCost) × qty. Partial fills are handled correctly. If no price bar exists for a trade date, the nearest prior bar is used (indicated by a warning in the hover tooltip).

Flexible sheet names

If your workbook uses custom sheet names (e.g. OHLC, Blotter) the Sheet & Column Mapping panel appears automatically so you can select the correct sheets and map columns.
